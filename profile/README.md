# Hi there!  We're CouncilTech Solutions

##  About Us

CouncilTech Solutions is an IT Systems Training and Support company founded in 2023. We specialize in helping seniors and underserved adults navigate the digital world safely and confidently.

**Our Mission:** To empower those often overlooked in technological advancements with the knowledge and tools they need to thrive in an increasingly connected world.

##  What We Do

- **Cybersecurity Education** — Teaching safe online practices, password management, and threat awareness
- **IT Support** — Remote assistance for computers, smartphones, tablets, and audio/video setup
- **Ongoing Monitoring** — Password health tracking and dark web breach detection
- **Workshops & Presentations** — Interactive sessions on AI tools, online privacy, and digital safety

##  Our Core Principles
